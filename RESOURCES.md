## Official school directories and context

| Official resource | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [My School — ACARA](https://myschool.edu.au/) | Look up school profiles and published school data; school income is not a quoted tuition fee. |
| [Australian Schools List — ACARA](https://asl.acara.edu.au/home) | Check school and campus identities, sector and location; not a fee schedule. |
| [NESA registered non-government schools](https://www.nsw.gov.au/education-and-training/nesa/school-regulation/non-government-schools/registered-schools) | Verify NSW non-government registration and school identity; not a fee source. |

Registration directories confirm identities and school status; they do not establish current school fees. A directory covering an entire state does not expand this repository’s city boundary. My School may display terms before access to school profiles. The fee table links each school directly where a school-owned or designated source was recorded.

## Link review and fee dates

**Links reviewed: 19 October 2026 (Australia/Brisbane).** City guide headings and PDF download links were observed in a real browser; article and official directory page content was checked. The PDF documents were not re-audited page by page. This is a link review, not a fresh check of school fees. The underlying fee observations remain July 2026 research.

If a link moves, use the linked organisation’s own navigation and submit a correction. Do not replace a missing school-owned fee schedule with an unrelated third-party estimate.
