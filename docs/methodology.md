# How to read these Markdown fee tables

This collection is maintained by [Airbotix](https://airbotix.ai/). Private means non-government, including Catholic and Independent schools. Fee amounts are not school-quality rankings.

## Scope and freshness

Source checks date from July 2026. Publishing or changing Markdown on 19 October 2026 does not reverify the source. A linked fee page might since have changed, been removed or switched to 2027. Read its date, domestic/international applicability and relevant school year before using it.

This is a partial research snapshot. National school/campus deduplication is outstanding. ACT, NT, Tasmania and WA have no imported records; regional areas in the other states are incomplete. Adelaide primary contains priced rows only. Some pending research rows lag more complete parent guides. Some Sydney secondary rows have a readable source but no extracted secondary price. These gaps are retained explicitly rather than guessed.

## Table columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| School / campus and source | Public name and locality; a link points to the school-owned or school-designated fee source where one was recorded |
| 2026 annual amounts | Historical year-level amounts, narrative year ranges or entry-year summary; includes source-specific compulsory charges in some records |
| Other charges and conditions | Extracted levies, entry fees, deposits, credits and exclusions; not automatically additive to the annual amount |
| Historical review | Original source review date and accessibility status; source readability does not mean the amount was extracted |

K, Prep, Reception and Foundation denote the initial primary year as reported by the source; Y1–Y12 denote year levels. All amounts are AUD. Unknown prices must not become zero. Refundable bonds or credited deposits are not automatically permanent costs. Boarding, international fees, sibling discounts, scholarships, transport, uniforms, devices, excursions and optional subjects are not consistently included. Do not compute national averages, rankings or guaranteed first-year totals from this uneven sample.

## Source status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Readable 2026 source | Applicable public source found at review; year-level extraction may still be missing |
| Research pending | Review was not completed in this imported record |
| Older-year source only | An older schedule was found; older annual fees are not substituted for 2026 |
| Not publicly listed at review | Research did not find a full applicable public price at that date; not proof none exists today |
| Access restricted at review | Applicable source could not be openly read during research |

## Corrections

Submit the affected school/campus, stage, domestic fee year, year level, school source URL, amount basis and review date. Cite a school's own current schedule or designated host. Third-party directories are leads, not fee evidence. Do not bypass logins or upload private records or entire fee PDFs. Keep historical dates separate from publication dates, and 2026 observations separate from future years. Update the readable tables and coverage notes together.
